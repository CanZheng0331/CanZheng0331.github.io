My Research Interests

During my master's studies, I attempted to explore many research directions in wireless communication, covering signal processing, system optimization, and applications of AI/ML.

AI/ML for Wireless Communications

Period: 2024.12-Present

These researches aim to leverage deep learning (DL) and large language models (LLMs) to address challenges in millimeter-wave (mmWave) communications, such as beam prediction and localization.

  • Beam Prediction Combining multimodal perception/sensing (such as vision, radar, LiDAR and GPS) with DL models to achieve robust beam selection and reduce training overhead. Specifically, my focus is on sensing-assisted beam prediction, which is to proactively predict the optimal beam direction for future moments using perception information from past moments. I think it can be considered as the spatio-temporal beam prediction (i.e., BM Case 1 + Case 2 in 3GPP TR 38.843: Study on Artificial Intelligence (AI)/Machine Learning (ML) for NR air interface, Page 23).
    Formally, given only historical observations \(\mathbf{X}_{t-T_{\mathrm{History}}:t-1}\), we aim to predict the future beam sequence \(\hat{\mathbf{y}}_{t:t+T_{\mathrm{Prediction}}-1}\): $$ \hat{\mathbf{y}}_{t:t+T_{\mathrm{Prediction}}-1}= f_{\Theta}\!\big(\mathbf{X}_{t-T_{\mathrm{History}}:t-1}\big), $$ where \(f_{\Theta}(\cdot)\) denotes the learned predictor.

Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellite Communications

Period: 2024.7-Present

Why use cell-free (CF) concepts in LEO satellite communications, or rather, why do multi-satellite collaborative transmission? When considering direct-to-cell (D2C) users, i.e., single-antenna users, we found that the existing multi-beam satellite communication link budget is very tight and may not be able to meet quality of service (QoS) requirements, especially with the increasing number of users using satellite communication services. To meet greater capacity requirements and for fairness considerations, we adopted the concept of CF massive MIMO from terrestrial networks.

  • Cell-free NTN Uplink Optimization Until I finished this work, most of the work involved downlink optimization. However, we believe that uplink demand will also increase. In particular, to my knowledge, some telecommunications companies are considering uplink enhancements.

Next-Generation Waveforms (OTFS/AFDM)

Period: 2024.3-Present

These researches focuses on receiver design and performance analysis for novel modulation techniques such as orthogonal time-frequency space (OTFS) and affine frequency division multiplexing (AFDM), especially in high-speed mobile scenarios.

  • OTFS Channel Estimation Based on Sparse Recovery To address the sparsity of the OTFS system, we can construct the \(\ell_0\)-norm minimization problem: \(\min_{\mathbf{h}} ||\mathbf{h}||_0,\) where \(\mathbf{h}\) is the sparse channel vector rearranged from 2D delay-Doppler domain. Since this problem is NP-hard, we apply a variety of compresses sensing (CS) techniques to solve it.
