Biography

我于 2026 年 2 月获得高丽大学电气工程硕士学位，并于 2023 年 8 月获得西安交通大学信息工程学士学位。研究兴趣主要集中在面向无线通信系统的 AI/ML、OTFS/AFDM 以及低轨卫星通信。欢迎与我研究兴趣相近的本科生、硕士生和博士生交流合作。

I received my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Korea University in February 2026 and my B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University in August 2023. My research interests include AI/ML for wireless communication systems, OTFS/AFDM, and LEO satellite communications. I welcome students with overlapping interests to connect and collaborate.
