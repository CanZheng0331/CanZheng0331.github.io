新型波形：OTFS / AFDM

Next-Generation Waveforms (OTFS/AFDM)

状态：过往研究（2024.03 - 2026.02）

Status: Past (2024.03 - 2026.02)

研究动机Motivation

这一方向的研究动机来自高移动性通信中的基本难题：当信道在时频域快速变化时，传统接收机结构会变得脆弱，而系统又必须在复杂度可接受的前提下完成可靠估计与检测。OTFS 与 AFDM 之所以重要，是因为它们尝试用更适合高多普勒传播环境的表示方式来重构接收机问题。不过，我后来也逐渐意识到，这条线更像是理解高移动性物理层与稀疏结构利用的训练场，而不一定是我建议新同学长期投入的首选主线。

The motivation of this direction comes from a basic challenge in high-mobility communications: when channels vary rapidly in the time-frequency domain, conventional receiver structures become fragile, yet the system must still perform reliable estimation and detection under practical complexity constraints. OTFS and AFDM are attractive because they attempt to reformulate the receiver problem using signal representations better suited to high-Doppler propagation. At the same time, I later came to see this line more as a training ground for understanding high-mobility physical-layer design and sparse structure exploitation, rather than the main long-term direction I would recommend to new students.

数学问题模型Mathematical Problem

对于 OTFS，这条线的核心问题通常可以写成时延-多普勒域中的稀疏信道恢复。设接收向量为 \(\mathbf{y}\)，观测矩阵为 \(\mathbf{A}\)，待恢复信道向量为 \(\mathbf{h}\)，则基础形式可写为

For OTFS, the core problem can usually be written as sparse channel recovery in the delay-Doppler domain. Let \(\mathbf{y}\) be the received vector, \(\mathbf{A}\) the observation matrix, and \(\mathbf{h}\) the channel vector to be recovered.

$$ \min_{\mathbf{h}} \|\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{A}\mathbf{h}\|_2^2 + \lambda \Phi(\mathbf{h}), $$

其中 \(\Phi(\mathbf{h})\) 代表稀疏约束、平滑近似的 \(\ell_0\) 正则项，或在变量分裂后的等价约束。

Here \(\Phi(\mathbf{h})\) may represent a sparsity prior, a smooth approximation to the \(\ell_0\) norm, or an equivalent split-variable constraint. For AFDM, the focus shifts further toward low-complexity detection under high Doppler, where the goal is to approach strong receiver performance under practical complexity limits.
