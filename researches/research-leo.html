低轨卫星通信

Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellite Communications

状态：过往研究（2024.07 - 2025.07）

Status: Past (2024.07 - 2025.07)

研究动机Motivation

这一方向的核心动机来自 direct-to-cell 低轨卫星场景中的系统瓶颈。随着终端数增加，传统多波束架构在容量、链路预算和用户公平性上都很容易受限，尤其在上行方向，用户发射功率受限、卫星协作关系复杂、系统几何快速变化，使得“单小区、单卫星、独立服务”的思路越来越不够。我关注的是：能否把 terrestrial cell-free massive MIMO 的协作思想迁移到低轨卫星网络中，用多星协同来重构 uplink 资源分配问题。

The motivation of this direction comes from the system bottlenecks of direct-to-cell LEO satellite communications. As the number of terminals increases, conventional multi-beam architectures quickly run into limits in capacity, link budget, and fairness. The uplink is particularly challenging because user transmit power is constrained, satellite cooperation is nontrivial, and the geometry changes rapidly. I am therefore interested in whether the cooperative ideas of terrestrial cell-free massive MIMO can be transferred to LEO networks to redesign uplink resource allocation.

数学问题模型Mathematical Problem

从建模角度看，这一问题可以写成联合离散-连续优化：给定用户集合 \(\mathcal{U}\) 和卫星集合 \(\mathcal{S}\)，同时优化聚类变量 \(\mathbf{C}\) 与上行功率向量 \(\mathbf{p}\)，使系统效用最大化，例如加权和速率或兼顾公平性的目标：

From a modeling perspective, the problem becomes a joint discrete-continuous optimization. Given a user set \(\mathcal{U}\) and a satellite set \(\mathcal{S}\), the clustering variables \(\mathbf{C}\) and uplink power vector \(\mathbf{p}\) are optimized jointly to maximize a system utility such as weighted sum rate or a fairness-aware objective.

$$ \max_{\mathbf{C},\,\mathbf{p}} U(\mathbf{C},\mathbf{p}) \quad \text{s.t.}\; C_{s,u}\in\{0,1\},\; 0 \le p_u \le p_u^{\max}. $$

难点在于聚类与功率控制高度耦合，而且 NTN 的大尺度几何关系、覆盖变化和用户服务关系都会让搜索空间快速膨胀。

The main difficulty is that clustering and power control are strongly coupled, while the large-scale geometry, coverage dynamics, and user-service relationships in NTN make the search space explode quickly.

方法与研究路径Methods

围绕这个问题，我把多星协同上行建模成联合聚类与功率控制问题，重点不是只优化某一个局部模块，而是从系统层面寻找“协同接入”的结构。与很多文献主要研究 downlink 不同，我更关心 uplink enhancement，因为它更能体现 D2C 卫星系统未来的真实瓶颈。这条线目前公开成果主要集中在 ICUFN 2025 的工作上，但其核心价值在于提出一种 cell-free NTN uplink 的系统视角。

Around this problem, I modeled multi-satellite cooperative uplink transmission as a joint clustering-and-power-control task. The emphasis is not on optimizing a single local module, but on discovering a cooperative access structure from a system-level perspective. Unlike much of the literature centered on downlink optimization, I focus more on uplink enhancement because it better reflects future bottlenecks of direct-to-cell systems. The public output of this direction is currently concentrated in the ICUFN 2025 work, but its core contribution is a cell-free NTN uplink viewpoint.
