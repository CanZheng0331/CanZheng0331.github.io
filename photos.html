Dongguan, China

Theoretically, Guangdong Province is the hottest place in China, but I was there during winter, so the weather was very comfortable. As a visiting student at the GBU, it only took me about 20 minutes to walk to Songshan Lake Park every day. From there, I could see Huawei Ox Horn Campus.

Beijing, China

I spent almost two-thirds of my 24th year (my zodiac year) interning in Beijing. It is a city with many opportunities, but also a lot of pressure. The climate seems moderate, but the winters are windy, the springs are filled with willow catkins, and the summers are hot and humid. In any case, I will miss this time of hard work very much.
