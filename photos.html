中国东莞

Dongguan, China

理论上广东是中国很热的地区，但我去的时候正值冬天，气候很舒适。作为在大湾区大学访问的学生，我几乎每天都要步行二十分钟去松山湖公园，从那里还能看到华为欧洲小镇。

Guangdong is usually known for warm weather, but I visited in winter, so the climate felt very comfortable. During my visiting period at Great Bay University, I often walked about twenty minutes to Songshan Lake Park, where I could also see Huawei Ox Horn Campus.

中国北京

Beijing, China

我 24 岁这一年有将近三分之二的时间都在北京实习。这里机会很多，节奏也很快。冬天风大，春天有杨絮，夏天偏闷热，但这段全力投入工作的经历依然让我非常怀念。

I spent almost two-thirds of my twenty-fourth year interning in Beijing. The city offers many opportunities, but also a lot of pressure. Winters are windy, spring brings willow catkins, and summers can be hot and humid, yet I still miss this intense and rewarding period very much.
